    The sequence, every time

    1. Prepare. Permit, isolation list checked against the P&ID, everyone affected told.
    2. Shut down. Normal stop, and take away every remote or automatic start.
    3. Isolate. Operate the real energy-isolating devices: the breaker and the manual block valves.
    4. Lock and tag. One lock per isolation, keys in the lockbox, one personal lock per person.
    5. Release stored energy. Vent, drain, cool. Check it cannot build up again.
    6. Verify. Try the start. Prove zero at an open vent. Then, and only then, start work.
