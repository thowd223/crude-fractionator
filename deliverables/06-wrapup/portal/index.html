Basis and limits

  • The process model is a pseudo-component cut-point model with ideal VLE. Expect temperatures within about 5-10 C of a rigorous simulation. Confirm them in HYSYS/Petro-SIM on a full assay before detailed design.
  • Vendor-dependent items are marked TBD on the datasheets: heater burners, desalter internals, ejectors and packing.
  • Open items for detailed design: the global flare load study, a stack dispersion study, a satellite substation for the VDU (480 V cable runs of about 255 m), the C-101 overhead relief (4 T orifices), and full-vacuum design or a steam-out procedure for the vessels other than C-201.
  • LOPA frequencies and protection-layer credits are FEED judgements for the SIL workshop. The cost estimate is AACE Class 4.
