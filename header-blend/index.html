Technical basis for engineering review

Status: proof of concept. Stream flows and analyses are illustrative and the tool is not for design use. This section sets out every equation, constant, assumption and check so a process engineer can verify the method independently.

1. Calculation method

Steady-state, ideal-gas, molar mixing. No reaction, no phase change, no hold-up in the header.

Normalise each analysis y_ij = a_ij / Σ_j a_ij a_ij = reported mol%, stream i, component j Header component flow n_j = Σ_i F_i · y_ij F_i = stream molar flow, kmol/h Header total and mol frac F = Σ_i F_i , x_j = n_j / F Molecular weight MW = Σ_j x_j · MW_j kg/kmol Specific gravity (ideal) SG = MW / 28.964 Net heating value LHV = Σ_j x_j · ΔHc,net,j / 22.414 MJ/Nm³ (ΔHc in kJ/mol) Wobbe index (net basis) WI = LHV / √SG MJ/Nm³ Header heat supply Q = F · 22.414 · LHV / 1000 GJ/h H₂S ppmv = x_H2S · 10⁶ NG make-up (hold option) F_NG = (Q_base − Q_other) · 1000 / (22.414 · LHV_NG), F_NG ≥ 0

Q_base is the heat supply with every unit at its base flow. Q_other is the heat from all non-NG streams in the current case. Analyses that do not total exactly 100% are normalised, which is standard practice for GC results.

2. Reference conditions and assumptions

  • Normal conditions 0 °C, 101.325 kPa. Ideal-gas molar volume 22.414 Nm³/kmol. Compressibility Z = 1.
  • Heats of combustion at 25 °C, water as vapour (net / LHV). The 25 °C to 0 °C combustion-temperature correction is ignored; its effect is around 0.1%.
  • Wobbe index on a net (LHV) basis. Burner vendors and ISO 6976 usually quote gross (HHV) Wobbe. Absolute values differ from a gross-basis Wobbe, though the percentage swing between cases is comparable.
  • C4s are lumped as n-butane and C5+ as n-pentane. A heavier C5+ cut would raise MW and LHV slightly.
  • Air density for SG: 28.964 kg/kmol.
  • Limits shown (±5% Wobbe swing, 160 ppmv H₂S) are placeholders. Replace them with the site permit and the burner vendor's tolerances.

3. Component properties used

MW and molar net heats of combustion are standard thermochemical values entered by hand. They have not yet been checked against a cited reference. Check them against GPSA Engineering Data Book Section 23 (physical constants), ISO 6976 or the NIST Chemistry WebBook. The LHV column is calculated by the page from the ΔHc column.

4. Verification carried out

  • Independent re-implementation in Python (header-blend/verify.py, run with python3 verify.py). It matches this page to the precision shown for all three cases below.
  • Closure checks in every case: Σ x_j = 1 and Σ n_j = F, both to machine precision.
  • Hold option: with NG make-up on, the calculated heat supply equals the base case (2,160 GJ/h) exactly.
  • Hand check: equal moles of pure H₂ and CH₄ give MW 9.029 and LHV 23.30 MJ/Nm³, the simple averages of the pure-component values.

5. Not modelled in this prototype

  • Real-gas behaviour, hydrocarbon or water dew point, and condensation in the header or knock-out drums.
  • Transients. Each case is a new steady state; pressure swings and controller response during a trip are not represented.
  • Meter compensation. Orifice and other differential-pressure meters on fuel gas read wrong when MW changes. Plant flows must be density-corrected before mixing, and the correction itself shifts when a unit trips.
  • Components outside the list (CO, O₂, NH₃, mercaptans, COS, H₂O).
  • Analyser and lab uncertainty, and data reconciliation against the header meter.
